# Forslag til ny «Overview»-tekst på LinkedIn (Quad Invest AS)

Quad Invest AS is a private, founder-owned investment company based in Oslo. We invest growth capital in founder-led companies that have moved beyond the idea stage – with a product in the market, existing revenues and clear commercial momentum – as well as in real estate.

We focus on businesses with a clear business model, existing revenues and the ambition to scale further. We back experienced, entrepreneurial teams with strong product and market insight, and we prefer to act as a trusted capital partner rather than an operational investor.

We seek companies with a differentiated offering and sustainable competitive advantages. While industry-agnostic, our primary interest lies in technology-enabled businesses across software and hardware – where we have built companies ourselves.

We invest our own capital, typically NOK 0.5–5 million per company, usually as a minority shareholder and often alongside other investors. We invest with a clear plan for how value will be realised, but without a fixed timeline – we stay as long as it makes sense for the company and for us. We do not seek board seats: we are passive owners and available sparring partners.

Quad Invest is founded and led by Geir Elstad, founder and former sole owner of Nextron (2000–2024).

Tagline (under navnet): Patient capital and hands-on experience for founder-led companies.
